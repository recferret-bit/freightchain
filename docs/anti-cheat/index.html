Anti-Cheat

A ride ranks only if a person drove it for the whole shift. The game runs in the player's browser, so nothing it says is trusted on its own. The server cannot re-simulate the drive, so it does the next best things: it times the shift itself, demands a recording it can validate, holds every recording against its own rebuild of the ride's road – the holes are the server's, not the game's – measures how human the hands behind it look, and asks for things during the ride that only someone watching the screen can answer.

From cheapest to strongest

Nine layers

Each layer catches something the one before cannot. The first five reject on proof, the sixth refuses what should never be stored, the seventh and eighth mostly suspect, and the last one decides.

  1. 1

    Server-timed shift

    Driven seconds come from heartbeats and are capped by the wall clock. Distance may grow at most 28 m/s × 1.2 between beats. No heartbeat for 150 s ends the ride.

    Catches: an instant finish, a speed hack between beats, a vanished tab.

    fails the rideRide.applyReport · the stale-ride sweeper
  2. 2

    A checkable recording

    Every key / dash-button press and release (wall-clock ms) and a 10 Hz sample of the truck, uploaded every 15 s in SHA-256 hash-chained batches. The server recomputes every hash on arrival.

    Catches: an edited recording, a missing or re-ordered batch, one chained to another ride.

    rejecthash-mismatch · missing-batches · chain-broken · no-recording — client/js/telemetry.js, recording-checks.ts
  3. 3

    Physics bounds

    Under the governor (28 m/s × 1.05); no step the truck cannot make (× 1.2 + 2 m, also across batch boundaries); samples in time order and well-formed.

    Catches: speed hacks, teleports, junk rows.

    rejectover-governor · teleport · sample-out-of-order · bad-sample — checkSamples over the batch summaries
  4. 4

    Agreement with the ride

    The recording ends where the ride ended (30 m + 1 %, a lost tail allowed) and covers the driven time (under 50 % rejects, under 90 % flags). A truck that moved more than 50 m has inputs, and gas among them.

    Catches: a recording of a different drive, cut-out hours, a car driven with no hands.

    rejectrecording-mismatch · recording-incomplete · no-inputs · no-throttle-input · recording-gaps (flag) — checkAgreement, checkInputsPresent
  5. 5

    The server's road

    The road and its sinkholes are a pure function of the ride's seed. The server rebuilds them itself, hands that obstacle plan to the driver's game and to every replay, and measures each uploaded batch against it: every axle through every pit, the time off the tarmac, speed gained with the gas up.

    Catches: a game with the holes – or the off-road rule – taken out; a truck that speeds up with no throttle.

    reject / flagobstacle-ignored · obstacle-no-impact (flag) · offroad-ignored · accel-without-gas (reject / flag) — domain/road, telemetry/world-facts.ts, verdict/world-checks.ts
  6. 6

    Budgets per ride

    Game seconds ≤ wall × 1.05 + 30, batches ≤ wall ÷ 5 + 10, bytes ≤ wall × 4 kB + 256 kB; per batch 96 kB, 400 samples, 2000 events; plus the API rate limits.

    Catches: a made-up recording uploaded faster than real time, storage floods.

    refused · 400Ride.recordingAllowance · UploadBatchHandler (the chain stops at a refused batch)
  7. 7

    How human the hands look

    Per key: holds that barely vary (spread under 8 % over 6+ holds). The 3 most common press-to-press gaps (1 ms buckets) covering more than 60 %. More than 30 % of presses released within 10 ms. No opinion under 8 presses; coarse browser clocks are detected and the two checks they break are skipped.

    Catches: macros, key-spamming scripts, synthetic events.

    flag onlyuniform-holds · repeated-timings · instant-presses — human-checks.ts
  8. 8

    Challenge channel

    The heartbeat can name a challenge from CHALLENGE_KINDS with a 20 s window. The table is empty today, so nothing is issued. A new kind is a row in that table. The cab handler shows whatever the DTO names. The judge looks for that press in the recording.

    Catches (when a kind is live): a bot driving the real game without reading the screen; a script reading the heartbeat and pressing at once.

    idlechallenges-ignored (≥ 50 % of ≥ 3 missed) · challenge-missed (flag) · challenge-reflex (median answer under 80 ms, flag) — ride-challenge.ts, challenge-checks.ts, client/js/prompt.js
  9. 9

    A person decides

    Flagged shifts wait in the admin queue. Accept, reject and re-judge are signed by the admin's wallet for that exact ride and decision, and audited. A late upload never overturns a decision.

    Catches: everything the statistics only suspect.

    the final wordadmin.<apex> · VerdictService.override — see the verified-play explainer, "Admin decisions"
Layer 5, closer

The server's road

The game draws a road the server never sees, so the server builds its own. The road's layout and its natural sinkholes are a pure function of the ride's seed: server/src/domain/road is a line-for-line port of the game's generator (road-layout.ts ⇄ client/js/net/network.js, road-plan.ts ⇄ net/obstacles.js). That obstacle plan is served at GET /api/watch/:id/road?from&to – public once the ride has started, at most 10 km per request – and it is the only source of holes for the driver's game and for every replay. The game's own roll is the offline path and the fallback for a stretch whose plan has not arrived yet; road-drift.spec.ts runs the game's own modules beside the port on 40 seeds × 60 km and fails on any difference.

What is measured

per batch, on arrival
  • Every axle through every pitFront (2.2 m ahead of the driver's eye), drive (−0.4 m) and trailer (−11.5 m), wheels ±1 m either side: where each axle crossed a planned pit, how far the nearer wheel was inside it, the speed and the hull before the pit and after it.telemetry/world-facts.ts · stored in the batch summary
  • Off the tarmacEvery spell with the truck outside the lanes, aprons and side roads of the server's layout (everything past the start junction).
  • Speed without gasEvery step where the speed rose although the pedal was up at both samples and no gas key went down in between (the input column).

How it is judged

rules v3
  • The game's damagePer axle entering a pit: hull % = dmg × (v / 28 m/s)², rear axles × 0.6, nothing under 3 m/s, nothing below 0.3 %. A sinkhole is dmg 14 – about 31 % for all three axles at 28 m/s.
  • A pass countswhen the wheel was at least 0.4 m inside the pit and the game's damage is at least 1 %. It is hit if the hull lost at least 25 % of that, otherwise ignored. A hole is hit if any axle hit it.
  • Two holes ignoredThe game never had them.obstacle-ignored · reject (one: obstacle-no-impact · flag)
  • Too long in the sandThe game ends a ride after 20 s off the road (draining twice as fast back on it); more than 20 × 1.25 + 2 = 27 s in a recording means that rule was taken out.offroad-ignored · reject
  • Faster without throttleThe road is flat: only the engine speeds the truck up. Steps of ≥ 0.15 m/s with the gas up – 2 m/s in total or 5 steps reject, fewer flag.accel-without-gas · reject / flag

Layer 8, closer

Challenge channel

The machinery that needs eyes on the screen when a kind is live. The cab handler is wired; kinds live in a table that is empty today, so the heartbeat sends no challenge. When a row is added, the game shows an amber banner with the action and the key; a dash button counts too. Pressing it is recorded like any other key, and the judge compares that time with the moment the server issued the prompt.

What the driver sees

20 s

The cab handler is wired (prompt.js). CHALLENGE_KINDS is empty, so the heartbeat sends challenge: null and no banner is shown. Add a row to that table to turn a kind on; the banner then shows whatever the DTO names.

What the judge does

from the recording
  • AnsweredThe first press of one of the prompt's codes between the issue time and the deadline. Its reaction is the gap after the issue time.scores.challenges · issued · answered · missed · tooFast · medianReactionMs
  • Ignored for most of the rideHalf or more of 3+ judged prompts missed: nobody was at the wheel.challenges-ignored · reject
  • A few missedFewer than that, or under 3 issued: a person looks.challenge-missed · flag
  • Answered before it could be readA median under 80 ms over 2+ answers. A person needs about 250 ms to read a banner; the one-way network delay only makes a real reaction read shorter, so this stays safe for slow links.challenge-reflex · flag
  • Still open at the endA prompt whose window had not closed when the ride ended is not judged.
What comes out

The verdict and every code

Any reject reason makes the shift rejected. Otherwise any flag makes it flagged. Otherwise it is verified. Only verified paid shifts rank, once VERDICT_ENFORCE is on; in shadow mode the verdict is shown and the boards ignore it.

Verified

Nothing found. The shift ranks on the paid leaderboard.

Flagged

Something looked off but proves nothing. The shift waits for a person and does not rank until accepted.

Rejected

The recording is missing, broken, impossible, at odds with the server's road, or the driver ignored the prompts. It does not rank. An admin can still reverse it.

Fairness rules

What keeps honest players safe

  • Statistics never rejectTiming checks only flag; a person reviews them before anything is decided.
  • A lost final upload only costs coverageThe distance the missing seconds explain is allowed, and the server keeps taking batches for 60 s after the end.
  • A missing fingerprint is not an editThe browser retries its digest once; a batch without one is kept and only flagged. A wrong fingerprint still rejects.
  • Prompts are forgiving by designFewer than 3 issued never reject; a prompt still open at the end is not judged; 80 ms sits far under a human reaction and a slow link cannot push a person under it.
  • Slow PCs and privacy browsers are not punishedInput times come from the browser's own events; rounded clocks are detected; OEM and IME keys with no code are accepted.
  • Going round a hole is never judgedA wheel that passed a pit by 0.4 m or more is "avoided"; a crawl through it (under 1 % of damage) is not judged; a wheel on the very edge is too close to call and is left alone. One hole without damage only flags.
  • The game drives on the server's holesThe driver's game takes its holes from the same plan the judge uses, and its own roll is drift-tested to be identical – an honest driver is never judged on a hole that was not on the screen.
  • Nothing lives foreverRecordings are deleted after 90 days (flagged ones kept until reviewed); the sign-in log with its IP addresses after 180 days.
  • Every verdict has reasons and a versionThe codes, what they measured and the rules version are stored, so a verdict can be explained and judged again.
  • Human decisions stickA late upload never overturns an admin's decision; a bulk re-judge keeps it too.

Limits

What this does not do

  • No re-simulation.A careful bot playing the real game with human-like timing and reading the prompts passes the automatic checks. The prompts raise the bar from "feed a plausible recording" to "run the real game and parse the screen or the heartbeat"; the reflex check catches the lazy version of that.
  • A faked hull loss is a hit.The road layer checks that the recording is consistent with the server's road, not that the client is honest: a modified game that drives over the holes AND writes the matching hull loss is a game that hit the holes. It raises the bar from "take the holes out" to "fake the damage exactly where the server's plan says".
  • Traffic and paid holes are the browser's.The cars are simulated in the driver's game and only recorded for replays – never judged. Holes spectators paid for are placed by the driver's game and are not judged either. Recordings from before rules v3 (or without the input column) skip the checks they have no data for.
  • The thresholds are first guesses.Including the prompt ones – interval, window, reject share, 80 ms. None has met real players yet; shadow mode is on until they have.
  • Assist is free-rides only.Half the hull wear and battery drain (the server refuses it on paid rides). The hole check wants a pass to lose at least a quarter of the normal damage; assist loses half, so honest assisted rides pass – a test pins both numbers.
  • A captcha is on the roadmap.Before a paid shift starts and before a prize is claimed — booked on the landing trip sheet, LEG 3. No vendor yet. A wallet farm can still take free rides; the five-per-day cap and the rate limits are the brake until then.
  • No per-ride clock offset.Reaction time is measured against the server's issue time. A bot on a slow link reads slower than it is; a clock-offset estimate from the heartbeats is the next step if that shows up.
  • The replay core is still the target.The design document's fixed-tick, fixed-point replay with an input ratchet and proof-of-render remains the long-term answer, not the build.
