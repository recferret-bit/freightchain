Organizer.** The FreightChain Pre-season (the "**Event**") is organized by an individual, [PLACEHOLDER: organizer name or public handle], contact [PLACEHOLDER: Telegram @handle / email] (the "**Organizer**"). **2. No purchase necessary. Entry only by completing tasks.** Nothing can be bought in the Event and no payment is required. **3. Dates (UTC).** Registration and tasks open Monday 2 November 2026. Review until Monday 16 November 12:00; appeals until Tuesday 17 November 12:00; payouts start Tuesday 17 November 2026. The game runs on the Solana **devnet** test network; devnet tokens have no value.

**4. Eligibility.** You must be **18 or older** (or the age of majority where you live, if higher) and a real person playing for yourself.
- **Prize-eligible:** residents of and persons located in Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Georgia, Moldova, Ukraine (except the occupied Crimea, Donetsk and Luhansk regions) and other locations not excluded below.

- **No VPNs or proxies** during your ranked attempt or at payout.

**5. How to enter.**
1. Connect a Solana wallet and accept these Rules (18+ confirmation).
2. Complete **all** mandatory tasks: (a) a verified MEDIUM or HARD test ride; (b) link your Telegram account and join the [PLACEHOLDER: channel] channel and the [PLACEHOLDER: chat] chat; (c) follow @FreightChainSol on X; (d) publish a public post on X (or a quote of our campaign post) saying that you take part in the FreightChain Pre-season, containing your personal code, and submit its link — this post also links your X account. The post and the follow must stay in place until the end of the review. Discord is optional and earns airdrop points only. Likes and plain reposts are not counted.
3. You then receive **exactly one ranked attempt** on the 1h Event board.

**6. One person, one attempt.** Each person may hold one wallet, one Telegram account and one attempt. Creating or using more than one wallet or Telegram account, sharing accounts, letting others play for you, bots, scripts, cheats or exploits are prohibited. **If one person is found behind more than one entry, all of that person's entries are disqualified.** Leaving the required channel or chat before the end of the prize week voids the attempt.

**7. Contest of skill.** Every ranked attempt drives the same Event route. Rank depends on driving performance: the share of the route completed before the first mistake, then fewer corrections at a clean finish. There are no random draws and no submission-time or wallet-address tie-breakers. Results that are fully equal **share the combined prize amounts of the places they occupy equally**. Unlimited free training rides are available.
