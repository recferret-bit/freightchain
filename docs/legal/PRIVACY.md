## Summary

- **No account, email or password is needed to play.** You sign in by connecting a Solana wallet and signing a message. Your **wallet address is your player ID**.
- **Blockchain data is public and permanent.** Ticket payments, prize payouts, TEN4 token transfers, achievement badges and other on-chain actions are recorded on the Solana blockchain. Anyone can see them, and **we cannot change or delete them**.
- **Identity checks happen only when you win.** Before we pay certain prizes we ask you to verify your identity through our provider Didit and to fill in a US tax form (W-9 or W-8BEN). We screen wallets against sanctions lists.
- **We protect fair play.** We record gameplay telemetry, compute a "trust score", and use network signals (a keyed, salted hash of your IP address prefix), wallet history and linked social accounts to detect cheating, bots and multi-accounting. **We do not use browser fingerprinting.**
- **We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.** We do not run third-party advertising trackers on the Services.
- **The Services are for adults 18+** (or the age of majority where you live, if higher) and are **not available** in the United Kingdom, the European Union/EEA, sanctioned countries and certain US states (see Section 11).

## 4. Public and on-chain information

- **On-chain data is public and immutable.** When you buy a ticket, receive a prize, claim or receive TEN4, receive an achievement badge (soulbound compressed NFT) or interact with our Solana programs, the transaction, your wallet address, amounts and timestamps are published on the Solana blockchain. This information is visible to anyone, may be indexed by third-party explorers and analytics firms, and **cannot be altered or deleted by us or anyone else**. Deletion requests (Section 9) cannot apply to on-chain data.
- **Airdrop allocations** are published as a Merkle root on-chain; the allocation list (wallet and amount) may be published so that anyone can verify it.
- **[CHANGED 2026-10-17]** **Public game data:** leaderboards and Convoy rankings show your wallet address (shortened) or your public .sol name, results and achievements. You can hide yourself from the Convoy referral leaderboard with the "hide me" switch in your profile; you are then shown as "hidden driver". Ride replays are not a live view. Rides in progress are not publicly watchable, and a replay is of a completed non-training ride only. A prize-pool ride's replay is public only after that pool's validation window ends. Replays show positions reconstructed by our servers, not your raw per-frame inputs.
- **[CHANGED 2026-10-17]** **Replays:** rides in progress are not publicly watchable. A ride replay becomes public only after the ride is completed and, for a prize-pool ride, after the pool's validation window ends. Replays show positions reconstructed by our servers, not your raw per-frame inputs or input timing.
- **[CHANGED 2026-10-17]** **Replay chat** messages are visible to everyone viewing that replay.
- Combining your wallet address with other information (for example a linked X handle you made public) may identify you. Please consider this before linking accounts or posting.
