01What TEN4 is

TEN4 is a coin with a hard cap. It is not the ticket and it is not the prize. Until launch the balance is points on the game ledger.

SOL buys the ticket. SOL pays the pool. A pool's prize maths do not change because TEN4 exists. HONK is the verb: burn 1 TEN4 to put HONK on the chat line.

Starting a ride mints nothing. A crash that never reached a checkpoint mints nothing. A free ride mints nothing. A flagged ride mints nothing. A rejected ride mints nothing.

The source of truth until TGE is the PostgreSQL ledger. The coin is not live on devnet. The numbers are in docs/spec/ten4.md.

03Checkpoints

Three bands of that ride's completeDist: 20%, 50%, 100%. A band is reached when distance + 1 >= completeDist × pct / 100. The extra metre keeps a finish the game calls complete on the 100% band.

Each band pays its own share of that tier's full mint. The shares are not cumulative weights. They sum to 100%.

05Verification

TEN4 is reserved only when the verdict is verified. Flagged pays 0. Rejected pays 0. Flagged is not a pass. Rejected is not a pass.

The reservation runs inside the verdict transaction, under the player row lock, after the verdict is saved. One row per ride. A 1h or 4h finish credits immediately. An 8h finish waits 48 hours: the emission and that day's caps are reserved, and the wallet is credited by a later crank. A non-verified rejudge before that deletes the reservation. A second pass that is still verified does not mint again.

No clawback after credit. If an admin later rejects a ride that already credited, the coins stay. An 8h ride that is still on hold can be taken back.

Before the verdict, a paid ride is a preview. A verified 8h that has not been released is pending. A released pass is minted. Flagged or rejected is withheld. A free ride is none.

Free rides mint nothing.

06Stream burn

On a running paid ride that can be watched, a signed-in wallet may burn 1 TEN4 to HONK. The chat line is the word HONK. The truck does not change. No hole, no night, no hull change.

Once per wallet, per ride, per 10 seconds. A free ride is refused. If the chat window refuses the line, the burn does not land. Not enough TEN4 is an error, not a server failure. The answer is the new balance and a pay of 1.

The gap caps one wallet at 6 burns a minute and 360 an hour. A verified finish funds that many honks, and that many seconds of mashing at the cap.

Time at the cap is how long that mint lasts at one burn every 10 seconds.

07Cab visuals

Burn 65 TEN4 to mint a soulbound cab visual. If the item is gold, the burn is 260 TEN4. This replaces promo-code colours. Tickets and extra free rides can still be promo codes. A promo that tries to grant a colour is refused.

The garage equips what the burn unlocked. Already owned burns nothing. Defaults stay free: the classic wheel, the red truck, the stick shifter, the white die, the orange needle, the hood spear, the stock dash, the amber lamps, and the black phone. Everybody owns them. An old promo unlock still fits. There is no transfer.

Four more slots use the same count: a hood ornament, the dash fascia, the warning-lamp bezels, and the phone frame. Six each. The shifter was already a slot. Its stick changes with the knob.

The game ledger is the cab. A burn in the garage writes the entitlement in the game. A local program can also mint one frozen token for that part, supply 1, with no thaw. That program is not deployed, and the part is not live on devnet. It is not a cNFT.

10Who it is for

Two people already in the game. Someone watching a paid ride, who can burn 1 TEN4 to put HONK on the chat line. Someone who wants a cab colour, who burns TEN4 in the garage instead of redeeming a colour code.

Say what the cab does. SOL is the ticket and the prize. TEN4 is minted by verified paid checkpoints, up to a cap, and a missed checkpoint mints nothing. A honk on a stream burns 1 TEN4 and prints the word HONK. The truck does not move. A colour is a burn recorded in the garage. The quote is on the ride before the verdict. The mint is on the ride after a pass. Flagged and rejected pay 0.

Do not say it is a prize. Do not say it yields. Do not say "buy TEN4 to earn". Do not call it an ICO. Devnet SOL has no value. The coin is not launched on a DEX in this change. Do not claim an audit. Do not claim the coin or the cab visual is live on devnet.

The channels are the ones the game already has. The watch page, where the burn is a chat line. The garage, where the price is in TEN4 and the part equips. The end-of-ride line, where the quote and then the mint are stated. One clip of a honk on a stream is enough to show that the truck does not react.

Do not send people to a market. The local pool is not a listing. Until a paid ride has minted on the ledger, the TEN4 a player can point at in the game is a number in that ledger.

11Liquidity pool

The local program pairs 15,000,000 TEN4 with 50 SOL. The fee is 0.25%. The LP is burned in the initialize transaction. There is no withdraw. 50 SOL is the opening deposit, not a dollar price.

The team bucket is locked at 12-month cliff plus 24 months linear. Emission, airdrop, referral, treasury, and team are separate vaults. A real launch should use a standard Raydium CPMM or Meteora DAMM v2 at 0.25–1% with the LP burned. This program is the local mirror.

That program is not deployed. There is no DEX listing. Until TGE the balance a player sees is ledger points.

SOL remains the prize. TEN4 is not a prize and not an offer. Devnet SOL has no value. The local program has not been audited and is not deployed.
