Issues surfaced in Pass 1

  • [CRITICAL] Fungible mint freeze authority is not explicitly revoked in spec (unspecified)On Solana, an active or unspecified freeze authority on the fungible mint allows freezing any user's Associated Token Account (ATA) or DEX LP pool. Automated scanners (RugCheck, Jupiter, Birdeye, Photon, BullX) flag the token as a honeypot risk. (Note: freezing supply-1 soulbound cab part tokens is fine, but the fungible token mint itself must explicitly state `freezeAuthority = null`).
  • [HIGH] Split-brain ledger vs. on-chain mint architecture (capped_ledger_or_pda)The spec states that the PostgreSQL game ledger is the source of truth ('There is no on-chain SPL mint... The ride emission, the other 98,000,000, stays on the game ledger and is not deposited into the pool'), while a separate local program holds a 2,000,000 HONK pool. Splitting 98% of supply onto an off-chain database table and 2% into an isolated local AMM creates two disconnected balances and prevents trustless SPL composability.
  • [MEDIUM] Metadata mutability / off-chain URI permanence gap (updateAuthority: unspecified, URI: unspecified)Mutable metadata or unspecified/local JSON hosting allows post-launch rug-renaming or broken token logos on Jupiter and Solscan.
  • [HIGH] Redeemable LP Locker Cliff at Day 365 ('holder can redeem them for their share of what the pool still holds')While the spec commendably sets 0% Team and 0% Investor allocations ('No team allocation'), the entire genesis liquidity pool (2,000,000 HONK + 10 SOL) is only time-locked for 365 days, after which the LP holder can withdraw 100% of remaining SOL and HONK reserves—draining all pool liquidity to zero at Month 12.
  • [HIGH] Unbalanced Genesis / TGE Circulating Float (2%)Ultra-Low Genesis Float (2% in genesis pool vs. 98% unemitted ride overhang — a 49x dilution multiplier). Selling just 2% of ride emissions (2,000,000 tokens = 1,000 8h finishes) into a 2% genesis pool drains 50% of the SOL reserve and crashes spot price by 75%.
  • [HIGH] Zero-Fee (0 bps), Shallow (10 SOL) Local Constant-Product Pool (Local Constant-Product Pool (10 SOL, 0% fee, undeployed))Pairing 2,000,000 HONK with only 10 SOL at a 0% swap fee ('The out is the constant product, rounded down. The fee is zero') means: (1) a single 5 SOL buy moves the price 2.25x and pulls 33.3% of all HONK out of the pool, (2) 0 bps swap fee allows frictionless MEV sandwiching with zero LP fee accumulation, and (3) `index.html` omits the 25 HONK weather (`snow`/`clear`) burn present in `SPEC.md`.
  • [MEDIUM] Incomplete viral loop across Spectator Chaos, Clips, and Solana BlinksSolana attention markets require an immediate visual hook and frictionless social sharing. In `index.html`, Section 10 restricts distribution to existing players ('Two people already in the game... Do not send people to a market') and omits `/r/:id` Solana Blinks cards and clip loops beyond a static 'HONK' chat word.
  • [MEDIUM] Go-To-Market funnel & creator challenge strategy under-specifiedSection 10 ('Who it is for') intentionally limits reach to existing watch-page and garage users without a free-ride-to-paid-ride conversion hook or creator stream gauntlet loop.

(2) An 8h finish ($50 ticket -> 2,000 HONK = 40 HONK/$) yields 2.0x more HONK per dollar than a 1h finish ($5 -> 100 HONK = 20 HONK/$), concentrating emission capture in 8h grinders.
  • [HIGH] Fixed-Token Sink Pricing & Paid-Ride Spectator Griefing (`LIVE_EFFECTS` Bypass Paying 0 SOL to Driver)Adversarial Stress Test: (1) Section 06 states that burning 10 HONK (day/night) and 25 HONK (snow/clear in SPEC.md) on a live paid ride 'is not a donation and it does not pay the driver... One verified 8h ride (2,000 HONK) funds 200 night-time flips (over 66 minutes of continuous 20s effect windows), letting rival spectators keep a competitive paid driver in permanent night/blizzard while paying the driver 0 SOL. (2) Fixed prices (1 / 10 / 25 / 65 / 260 HONK) lack TWAP adjustment under 10x price rallies or 80% drawdowns.
  • [HIGH] One-Shot Garage Ceiling (4,485 HONK Max Per Wallet) & Terminal 98M Emission Cap ShutoffAdversarial Stress Test: (1) The entire 45-part Garage costs 4,485 HONK total (37 ordinary x 65 = 2,405 + 8 gold x 260 = 2,080), and 'Already owned burns nothing.' A driver who finishes just two 8h rides and one 4h ride (4,500 HONK minted) unlocks 100% of the Garage forever—after which their Garage burn demand drops to zero! Even if 1,000 drivers buy the entire Garage, that burns only 4,485,000 HONK (4.58% of the 98M ride emission cap). (2) Because 'A burn does not raise the cap and is not reminted,' once 98,000,000 HONK is emitted (e.g., 49,000 8h rides), the emission counter permanently closes, leaving future verified riders with 0 HONK forever.
  • [MEDIUM] High Token Velocity ('Hot Potato' Syndrome) Without Time-Weighted Holding SinksAdversarial Stress Test: Consumable sinks (buying a garage paint job once or dropping a pothole) create momentary transaction volume, not sustained token lockup (`M = PQ / V`). Without a reason to hold across shifts, earned tokens are immediately market-sold.
