# HONK – the capped coin

Agent reference. Read `docs/honk/SPEC.md` before changing a number. The numbers are locked. Do not retune them. The public restatement is `docs/honk/index.html`.

## In short

- **What it is.** HONK is a capped meme coin. SOL still buys the ticket and pays the pool. It is minted only when a paid ride's verdict is `verified`, at distance checkpoints. Starting a ride mints nothing. A crash that never reached a checkpoint mints nothing. Free rides mint nothing. In this change the PostgreSQL ledger is the source of truth.
- **Spec.** `docs/honk/SPEC.md`.
- **Domain.** The quote and the bands live in `server/src/domain/honk/tokenomics.ts`. The mint runs inside the verdict transaction, under the player row lock, after the verdict is saved. Idempotent on `rideId`. Flagged and rejected mint nothing. A later rejection of a verified ride is not clawed back.
- **Checkpoints.** Bands of that ride's `completeDist`: 20% / 50% / 100%, shares 15 / 25 / 60 of the tier's full mint (not cumulative weights). A band is reached when `distance + 1 >= completeDist * pct / 100`. The multiplier is not the hour count: 1h ×1 = 100 HONK, 4h ×5 = 500, 8h ×20 = 2,000. Bands: 1h 15 / 40 / 100, 4h 75 / 200 / 500, 8h 300 / 800 / 2,000 (cumulative). Verified finishes to emit 98,000,000: 980,000 of 1h, 196,000 of 4h, or 49,000 of 8h. Free, unknown tier, or `completeDist <= 0` → 0.
- **Supply.** Max 100,000,000. Decimals 6. Genesis 2,000,000 (2%) is the liquidity pool only, not ride-minted. Ride emission cap 98,000,000. Burns do not raise the cap and are not reminted. A mint that does not fit the remainder mints the remainder, then the cap is closed.
- **Stream burn.** 1 HONK on a running paid ride that can be watched. The chat line is `HONK`. That line is still chat-only: no effect on the truck. Once per wallet per ride per 10 seconds. A refused chat line means the burn does not land. Burning 10 HONK (`POST /api/watch/:id/honk/time`, `{ phase: "day" | "night" }`) posts kind `honk` on the same 20s effect window as a NIGHT/DAY donation, one game effect at a time. It is not a donation and does not pay the driver. The driver's game moves the clock; the replay shows the new hour. Burning 25 HONK (`POST /api/watch/:id/honk/weather`, `{ weather: "snow" | "clear" }`) posts kind `honk`, effect `snow` or `clear`, on that same window. It is not a donation. `LIVE_EFFECTS` does not gate the time or weather burn (that switch still gates donation obstacles). A free ride, the driver's own ride, a mute, or a ride that is not live is refused before any debit.
- **Cab visuals replace promo-code colours.** Names, descriptions, and prices are `economy.HonkPart` rows. 65 HONK, or 260 if the item is gold (30% above the first prices). The current 45 parts burn 4,485 (37 × 65 + 8 × 260). The burn charges the row. Soulbound entitlement in the game. Ticket and extra-free-ride promos stay. `kind: 'cosmetic'` is refused (`visuals_are_honk`). Existing promo unlocks stay wearable. The garage can preview every part, locked ones included. APPLY still saves only owned parts.
- **Not a chain mint in production.** The PostgreSQL ledger is the balance. The server holds no HONK mint key. When `HONK_PROGRAM_ID` is unset, a burn is ledger-only and no signature is required. When it is set, the wallet signs first and the server confirms that transaction before the ledger debit. The coin and the visual are not live on devnet.
- **Achievements.** Family `honk`, name HONK. Ids: `honk-haul` (First Mint, one verified paid mint), `honk-haul-2` (Checkpoint Hauler, five verified paid finishes), `honk-haul-3` (Long Haul Mint, twenty-five verified paid finishes). Do not retune an existing id. A rejected ride has no mint row, so it cannot earn these.
- **Local program only.** `freightchain_honk` (`GctCYSHbCnHN8sE1LjUL9cmSpFd5mcVdfaumRcaVWZZx`) mints the 2,000,000 genesis into its own pool. SOL and HONK swap both ways at the constant product with no fee. The fungible mint cannot freeze a holder. LP stays in the program locker for 365 days, then the holder can redeem it for its share of the reserves. It is not deployed, not on devnet, and not a Raydium pool. Prize-pool maths are unchanged. No free-ride mint, no ICO, no team allocation, no second mint, no buyback. Ride emission is a separate mint under the cap and is not deposited into this pool.
- **Local chain.** `docs/agents/honk-program.md` and `docs/agents/honk-localnet.md`. The ledger is still the game balance.
