# Free run levels, helpers, free rides – detail

Agent reference, moved verbatim out of `AGENTS.md` (which keeps only the map and the hard rules and is loaded into every agent session); an "In short" block, where there is one, is a summary. Read the file for a topic before changing it; keep it current like AGENTS.md ("Maps stay current").

## In short

- **The free run – three LEVELS** (`server/src/domain/rides/ride-trip.ts` `FREE_LEVELS`): `POST /api/rides` `level?: 1|2|3` (default 1; a level on a paid tier 409 `level_free_only`): **1 EASY** `free-1c` 3 min / 1512 m (four lanes, winding, TWO crossroads) · **2 MEDIUM** (was CROSSROADS) `free-2c` 5 min / 3360 m (the depot turn, 3 crossroads, a fork) · **3 HARD** `free-3c` 30 min / 20 160 m (`free-3b` is frozen) (full road: 6 crossroads, 2 forks, a highway run, the sharpest bends, NO assist and NO tutorial – 409 `assist_not_allowed` / `tutorial_not_allowed`, the game forces both off). Each level is one hardcoded country (EASY Australia · Stuart Highway `stuart`, MEDIUM USA · Route 66 `route66`, HARD Austria · Italy · Brenner Pass `brenner` – `client/js/free-countries.js`); the server stamps that route and re-choosing the level moves it. Shift = `FREE_RIDE_SECONDS` (default 180 = EASY) × 1 / 5⁄3 / 10 (whole seconds). The levels were retuned on NEW trip ids (2026-10-11, twice): the first round `free-1` / `-2` / `-3` and the second round's `free-1b` / `-2b` are frozen (replays, history – `levelOfTrip` still maps them to their level) but ranks on no board; the exact crossroads counts come from the `xroad` / `open` patterns. A free ride ENDS BY ITSELF at the line. Live stream watching and chat/tips are disabled for running free rides (403 `free_ride_no_stream`; omitted from `/api/live` `rides`); finished free rides remain watchable as replays. Ordering again re-chooses the READY free ride in place without a credit (`readyFreeRide` in `/api/me`; `/start` refuses a re-chosen one, 409 `ride_rechosen`). One free leaderboard per level. Achievements `free-easy` / `free-crossroads` / `free-hard`. **Trips**: the server stamps `Ride.trip` on every ride so a replay rebuilds the same road; a trip id is FROZEN once rides carry it – retune with a new id (`client/js/trips.js`); `holesFrom` and `road` are JUDGED and mirrored in `road-rules.ts` (`TRIP_HOLE_FIRST` / `TRIP_ROADS`). Skills `trip-design`, `level-check`.
- **Free-ride helpers – TUTORIAL + ASSIST** (free tier only, the lobby's two checkboxes, `Ride.tutorial` / `Ride.assist`; a paid tier refuses either with 409): tutorial = `ui/tutorial.js` (each step waits for the driver; negative cases in `trouble()` – add one when players get stuck; nothing may flash), starting a ride with it on sets `Player.tutorialPassedAt`; assist = `ui/assist.js`, hull damage + battery drain ×`CFG.ASSIST` 0.5 + tips; the server mirror `ASSIST_HULL_DAMAGE` must stay above the verdict's `PASS_DAMAGE_SHARE` (`world-checks.spec.ts`). Both draw through the LEAF `js/guide.js` → `cockpit/guide.js` (never in replays); off they change nothing (`npm run trace:diff` identical). Achievement `on-my-own`. Bare `play.html` orders the real EASY ride (tutorial and assist on). `?sim=1` is the tool sim.
- **Training rides are unlimited.** They are not ranked, not on a prize board, do not mint TEN4, and cannot be watched live (`free_ride_no_stream`). Statistics and achievements still count. The old balance columns and the settings `free_on_signup` / `free_rides_daily_max` stay; the midnight grant does not run and ordering a training ride does not spend a credit. `POST /api/auth/guest` is how a visitor with no wallet plays EASY: a throwaway public key, progress does not merge. `?sim=1` keeps the local `free-g` sim for tools.
- **Weekly granted ticket**: one paid ticket per wallet per prize week (`weekly_ticket_tier`, default 1 hour). 18+ and prize geo. Expires Sunday 12:00 UTC, does not stack, uses a paid ranked slot, can be watched, does not mint TEN4. See `docs/prize/SPEC.md`.

## Detail

- **The free run – three LEVELS**: a free ride is ordered at a LEVEL (`POST /api/rides` `level?: 1|2|3`, default 1, anything else 400; a level on a paid tier is 409 `level_free_only`; asking again re-chooses the READY free ride's level + helpers on the same ride id – without a `level` it keeps the ride's own; the game's `POST /api/rides/:id/start` sends the `trip` it built its road from and a ride re-chosen since is refused, 409 `ride_rechosen`, the game reloads), each its own trip, shift and finish line (`server/src/domain/rides/ride-trip.ts` `FREE_LEVELS`: shift = `FREE_RIDE_SECONDS` (default 180 = EASY's length) × its multiple, finish = K × 28 m/s × that; paid stay K 0.5). The free levels (third round, 2026-10-11) – each level's road is an EXACT list of 500 m stretches (`road.first`: `xroad` = one crossroads, `open` = none, plus `fork` / `highway`; net/patterns.js says why the count is exact, `road-drift.spec.ts` proves it per trip): **1 EASY** (`free-1c`, × 1 = 180 s, K 0.3 → 1512 m: TWO crossroads (four-way, empty side roads) on four lanes, the straight start, bends × 3 – on Stuart Highway R ≥ ≈ 330 m –, no sinkhole within reach – holes from 4000 m) · **2 MEDIUM** (`free-2c`, × 5⁄3 = 300 s, K 0.4 → 3360 m: the depot STOP line + right turn, a crossroads, a FORK, then 2 more crossroads, bends × 1.35, traffic / cross traffic × 0.8, hole chance × 1.5, holes from 400 m) · **3 HARD** (`free-3c`, × 10 = 1800 s, K 0.4 → 20 160 m; `free-3b` is frozen at 900 s: 6 crossroads, 2 forks and a highway run, bends × 1.6, traffic × 1.4, hole chance × 2, and NO ASSIST – the server refuses it, 409 `assist_not_allowed`, the game forces it off). The first round (`free-1` 60 s / 420 m · `free-2` 3 min · `free-3` 10 min) and the second round's `free-1b` (3 min, 1 crossroads) / `free-2b` (6 min, 4 crossroads) are frozen: those rides replay on their own roads and keep their level, but the boards list only the new trips. `GET /api/tiers`' free row lists `levels: [{level, trip, name, seconds, completeDist, assist}]`; `RideDto.level` / `WatchRideDto.level` (null = a paid ride, or one on the old `free` trip). The lobby's level picker remembers the pick (`localStorage` `freightchain.level`). Each level chip shows that level's country, road and a short description, plus its nominal time and distance (HARD: `30:00 · ≥ 20.16 km` – a shift completes at the distance, the time is only its nominal length); nothing under the picker repeats the time. The country is hardcoded (`client/js/free-countries.js`) and is the route the server stamps (`FREE_LEVELS.route`), so the drive is that country. The start hour still comes from the seed. Paid rides still take route and hour from the seed (`worldFor`). TUTORIAL and ASSIST are disabled on HARD (each block stays readable, crossed by a stop-sign banner). Achievements `free-easy` Learner's Permit ①, `free-crossroads` Crossroads ② (for MEDIUM), `free-hard` Long Haul ③ (`Facts.completedFreeLevels`). New free rides default to level 1 (`TRIP_OF_TIER.free = 'free-1c'`); the old `free` trip (one minute, the straight start on the default layout) stays frozen for the rides that carry it. **Trips**: the server stamps the trip id on every new ride (`Ride.trip`, in the ride + watch payloads; null = a ride from before trips = 'classic') so a replay rebuilds the road the driver had: a trip id is FROZEN once rides carry it, retune with a new id (`client/js/trips.js`: `start: 'straight'` – the depot road runs straight on into the main road, no STOP line, no right turn, no traffic coming down it; the bends fade in over 150 m after it –, bends, traffic, calm cars, cross traffic, roadside density – client-only levers –, and two JUDGED knobs mirrored by the server: `holesFrom` (`road-rules.ts` `TRIP_HOLE_FIRST`) and `road` = the layout – `first` patterns, pattern `weights`, × `holeChance` (`TRIP_ROADS` / `tripRoadOf`; `new RoadLayout(seed, trip)`, `roadPlanFor(seed, ride.trip)`, drift-tested per trip); the old trips – classic / free / 1h / 4h / 8h, paid included – keep the default layout, unchanged) and a free ride ENDS BY ITSELF at the line with the green win stamp (`winFx`, `ui/fail-fx.js`). The free leaderboard is ONE PER LEVEL (`GET /api/leaderboard?board=free&level=1|2|3`, no level = 3 HARD, a level on a paid board = 400; index `Ride (status, trip)`): it ranks the completed free rides on that level's trip – the old `free` trip, the retired `free-1` / `-2` / `-3` / `-1b` / `-2b` / `free-3b` and classic rides rank on none; the lobby's FREE RIDES board has EASY / MEDIUM / HARD under it (HARD by default, the pick in `localStorage` `freightchain.board-level`). The lobby lists the top 100 (25 a page) with distance, time driven, the hull left on the furthest ride and shifts, and sorts by any of those. YOUR PLACE is the signed-in rider's distance rank on this round, all time and each level. A READY free ride (ordered, not started – its credit already spent) is `readyFreeRide { id, level, trip, tutorial, assist }` in `/api/me` (and the sign-in profile): the lobby keeps START on at 0 credits (`START FREE RIDE · ready`, its level + checkboxes preselected) and ordering again hands back that same ride, re-chosen, without a credit. Paid trips start at the depot's STOP line with the right turn and get harder 1 h → 4 h → 8 h (bends × 1 / 1.2 / 1.4, traffic × 1 / 1.4 / 1.8). Tuning: the `trip-design` skill.
- **Free-ride helpers – TUTORIAL + ASSIST** (free tier only; the lobby's free card has two checkboxes, `POST /api/rides {tier:'free', tutorial, assist}`; a paid tier refuses either with 409): stored on the ride (`Ride.tutorial` / `Ride.assist`, in `RideDto` – the game reads them from `GET /api/rides/:id`; offline `play.html?tutorial=1&assist=1`), a ready free ride is re-chosen in place when the lobby asks again. **Tutorial** = a dialog over the gameplay (`client/js/ui/tutorial.js`: ignition · parking brake · 1st gear + clutch · moving off · shifting up · steering · lights + windscreen · hull + crashes; each step waits for the driver to DO it, SKIP / END TUTORIAL; while it is open `guide.hold` keeps a trip that ends at the line from completing – but only until the line: reaching it cuts the lesson off, and the line ends the ride at once, offline and online – a shift is complete the moment its distance is covered, there is no minimum time (the server's `Ride.finish` judges the distance alone; the speed governor bounds how fast it can be reached)). The tutorial also has NEGATIVE CASES (`trouble()` in `ui/tutorial.js`: stall, grinding, neutral, redline, lugging, off-road, parking brake on, low battery, damage): the dialog turns amber with what happened + the next actions and highlights the fix; add a case there when players get stuck. Success is cheered too: a green windscreen banner (`cheer()` in `guide.js`, drawn by `cockpit/guide.js`) on each finished step, small wins (steered both ways, lights, wipers) and a recovered stall. Pacing is constants at the top of that file (`MIN_STEP` 3 s, `DONE_MS` 2.2 s, `WARN_AFTER` / `WARN_HOLD` / `WARN_GONE`) – nothing may flash. **Starting a ride with the tutorial on** sets `Player.tutorialPassedAt` (first time only, in `StartRideHandler` under the player lock; `tutorialPassed` in `/api/me`) – the lobby's TUTORIAL box is then off by default. **Assist** (`ui/assist.js`): hull damage and battery drain ×`CFG.ASSIST` 0.5 (= twice the durability; `hurt()`, cold wear, `updateBattery` – fatal hits stay fatal) and a tip on every control / indicator when it matters (parking brake, gear up / down, stall, lights at night, low battery, hull…). The assist checkbox is remembered in `localStorage` `freightchain.assist` (the tutorial default comes from the server). Assisted free rides rank on their level's free board like any other. Both draw highlights through `client/js/guide.js` (a LEAF: `guide.tips` + the per-frame `guide.rects` the dash fills in beside `uiBtns`) → `cockpit/guide.js` (green frames + tip bubbles; replays and `st.watch` never draw them). Achievement **On My Own** (`on-my-own`): a COMPLETED free ride driven without assist. The server mirrors the factor (`ASSIST_HULL_DAMAGE`, `ride-rules.ts`) and must keep `PASS_DAMAGE_SHARE` (verdict) below it – `world-checks.spec.ts` pins both and drift-tests the client's constant; changing either number means re-checking the obstacle verdict. Tests: `guide.scenarios.ts`, the headless checks (`tools/headless-test.mjs`), `e2e-lobby.mjs`.
- **Training rides**: unlimited. Ordering one does not spend `freeRides`. `nextFreeRideAt` is always null. The lobby chip says `training · unlimited`. `DailyFreeRideScheduler` is not registered; `GrantDailyFreeRidesHandler` returns `{ players: 0 }`. Columns `freeRides`, `freeDay`, `freeRidesMax` and settings `free_on_signup` / `free_rides_daily_max` stay so old numbers can still be read. Signed admin `POST /api/admin/players/free-rides` can still change the stored number; it does not gate a ride. `/api/me` also returns `guest` and `weeklyTicket` (`tier`, `expiresAt`, `rideId`). A visitor with no wallet plays EASY through `POST /api/auth/guest` (throwaway public key; achievements stay on that wallet). `?sim=1` is the only local sim. The old guest page (`?offline=1`, trip `free-g`) is retired. `free-g` remains only for `?sim=1`.
