01What HONK is

HONK is a meme coin with a hard cap. It is not the ticket and it is not the prize.

SOL buys the ticket. SOL pays the pool. A pool's prize maths do not change because HONK exists.

Starting a ride mints nothing. A crash that never reached a checkpoint mints nothing. A free ride mints nothing. A flagged ride mints nothing. A rejected ride mints nothing.

In this change the source of truth is the PostgreSQL ledger. There is no on-chain SPL mint and no new cNFT collection. The coin is not live on devnet. The locked numbers are in SPEC.md beside this page.

03Checkpoints

Three bands of that ride's completeDist: 20%, 50%, 100%. A band is reached when distance + 1 >= completeDist × pct / 100. The extra metre keeps a finish the game calls complete on the 100% band.

Each band pays its own share of that tier's full mint. The shares are not cumulative weights. They sum to 100%.

05Verification

HONK is minted only when the verdict is verified. Flagged pays 0. Rejected pays 0. Flagged is not a pass. Rejected is not a pass.

The mint runs inside the verdict transaction, under the player row lock, after the verdict is saved. One row per ride. A second pass that is still verified does not mint again. Any other status inserts nothing.

No clawback. If an admin later rejects a ride that already minted, the coins stay. Spent coins cannot be pulled back.

The rider sees the quote before the verdict, and the mint after a pass. Before the verdict, a paid ride is a preview: the amount credited is 0, and the quote is the sum of the checkpoints already reached. After a verified pass with a quote above 0, the ride is minted and the credited amount is what was minted. If the cap clipped it, that amount is the remainder. Flagged or rejected is withheld: credited 0, and the quote still shows what a pass would have paid. A free ride, or any ride that is not paid, is none: zeros, and no bands.

Free rides mint nothing.

06Stream burn

On a running paid ride that can be watched, a signed-in wallet may burn 1 HONK. The chat line is the word HONK. The truck does not change. No hole, no night, no hull change.

Once per wallet, per ride, per 10 seconds. A free ride is refused. If the chat window refuses the line, the burn does not land. Not enough HONK is an error, not a server failure. The answer is the new balance and a pay of 1.

The gap caps one wallet at 6 burns a minute and 360 an hour. A verified finish funds that many honks, and that many seconds of mashing at the cap.

Time at the cap is how long that mint lasts at one burn every 10 seconds.

07Cab visuals

Burn 65 HONK to mint a soulbound cab visual. If the item is gold, the burn is 260 HONK. This replaces promo-code colours. Tickets and extra free rides can still be promo codes. A promo that tries to grant a colour is refused.

The garage equips what the burn unlocked. Already owned burns nothing. Defaults stay free: the classic wheel, the red truck, the stick shifter, the white die, the orange needle, the hood spear, the stock dash, the amber lamps, and the black phone. Everybody owns them. An old promo unlock still fits. There is no transfer.

Four more slots use the same count: a hood ornament, the dash fascia, the warning-lamp bezels, and the phone frame. Six each. The shifter was already a slot. Its stick changes with the knob.

The game ledger is the cab. A burn in the garage writes the entitlement in the game. A local program can also mint one frozen token for that part, supply 1, with no thaw. That program is not deployed, and the part is not live on devnet. It is not a cNFT.

10Who it is for

Two people already in the game. Someone watching a paid ride, who can spend 1 HONK to put HONK on the chat line. Someone who wants a cab colour, who burns HONK in the garage instead of redeeming a colour code.

Say what the cab does. SOL is the ticket and the prize. HONK is minted by verified paid checkpoints, up to a cap, and a missed checkpoint mints nothing. A honk on a stream burns 1 HONK and prints the word. The truck does not move. A colour is a burn recorded in the garage. The quote is on the ride before the verdict. The mint is on the ride after a pass. Flagged and rejected pay 0.

Do not say it is a prize. Do not say it yields. Do not say "buy HONK to earn". Do not call it an ICO. Devnet SOL has no value. The coin is not launched on a DEX in this change. Do not claim an audit. Do not claim the coin or the cab visual is live on devnet.

The channels are the ones the game already has. The watch page, where the burn is a chat line. The garage, where the price is in HONK and the part equips. The end-of-ride line, where the quote and then the mint are stated. One clip of a honk on a stream is enough to show that the truck does not react.

Do not send people to a market. The local pool is not a listing. Until a paid ride has minted on the ledger, the HONK a player can point at in the game is a number in that ledger.

11Liquidity pool

A local program pairs the whole genesis, 2,000,000 HONK, with 10 SOL. The out is the constant product. The fee is zero. 10 SOL is the opening deposit, not a dollar price for HONK. The LP tokens sit in the program locker for 365 days.

No team allocation. No second mint. No market-making promise. No buyback. The ride emission, the other 98,000,000, stays on the game ledger and is not deposited into the pool.

That program is not deployed. There is no DEX listing, and no transaction is sent to Raydium. A ride still mints on the ledger.

SOL remains the prize. HONK is not a prize and not an offer. Devnet SOL has no value. The local program has not been audited and is not deployed.
