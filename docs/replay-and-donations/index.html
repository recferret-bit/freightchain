Replays & Donations

Any ride can be watched – live while it is on the road, or as a replay once it has ended. Nothing is filmed: the watch page rebuilds the driver's road from the ride's seed and replays the same 10-per-second recording that verified play already uploads. Signed-in spectators can write to the driver and send donations that change the driver's ride – night, day, or a hole in the road ahead.

The path of one watched ride

From the driver's wheel to a spectator's screen

1Driver's browser

Record

Every online ride records the truck 10 times a second (verified play). Two columns were added for spectators: the hour of the day and the cab's switches.

2Every 15 s of driving

Upload

The batch is sealed, hashed, chained and stored by the server – the same upload the verdict judges. There is no second stream.

3Watch page, every 4 s

Poll

GET /api/watch/:id returns the ride, the next batches the playhead needs, and the feed since the last poll.

4Every frame

Replay

The road is rebuilt from the seed + route; the truck's state is interpolated from the samples. Nothing is simulated.

5Signed in

Talk & donate

Chat lines and donations go into the ride's feed; the driver's game picks them up, shows them and applies the effects.

What a replay is

A rebuilt road and a recorded truck

The road is endless but deterministic: its whole layout – patterns, junctions, traffic lights, roadside props, the natural holes – is a pure function of the ride's seed and its route. The watch page gets both from the server, builds its own copy of the driver's road, and then writes the truck's state every frame from the recording instead of running the physics. Gauges, sway, the trailer, wipers, dust and lights all react exactly as they do for the driver, because they read the same state.

Playback

Live, behind, and replay

Following a live ride

● live
  • Target: 25 s behind the newest recorded secondOne 15 s batch plus its upload, with room for a late one.LIVE_LAG = 25 · client/js/watch.js
  • It eases, it doesn't jumpPlayback runs at 0.7× – 1.5× to settle on the target; only more than 60 s off makes it jump.
  • Waiting is honestIf the next batch is not there yet – the driver paused, backgrounded the tab, or their upload is slow – the last frame holds and the panel says so. A paused game records no game time, so a pause never shows as a gap.
  • Joining a long rideThe page fetches straight from the live edge (seq ≈ time ÷ 15 s) and holds until it is loaded.

Donations

Six buttons, three kinds of effect

Prices are a mock: they are shown and added up per ride, nothing is charged and no transaction is made. A donation can carry a note of up to 80 characters. The real version – a wallet-to-wallet transfer the server only counts once the chain shows it – is item F1 of docs/solana-features-plan.md.

💰 Tip 0.01 SOL

Money only. It shows on the driver's CB panel and in the feed. No effect on the ride.

not limited by the effect window – one per wallet per 3 s

🌙 Night 0.02 SOL

The driver's clock jumps to night (it fades in like the N key; the driver's own saved clock is untouched). Headlights matter from here on.

the replay follows from the clock column

☀ Day 0.02 SOL

The clock jumps back to day. Undoes a night – or a spectator's mercy.

the replay follows from the clock column

🕳 Potholes 0.02 SOL

Three small pits across the driver's lane. Each costs up to 1.8 % hull per axle at full speed – straddle them or slow down.

HOLES.cluster

🚧 Trench 0.03 SOL

A trench across the whole lane: up to 6 % hull per axle and 15 % of the speed at 100 km/h. Change lanes or slow right down.

HOLES.trench

⚠ Sinkhole 0.05 SOL

A lane-wide sinkhole with cones: up to 14 % hull per axle and 35 % of the speed at 100 km/h. Three axles go over it – at full speed it can wreck a damaged truck.

The life of one donation

Posted, applied, stamped, replayed

1Spectator

Post

Signed in, clicks SINKHOLE. POST /api/watch/:id/donations.

2Server

Check

Not your own ride, the ride is on the air, no other effect on it in the last 20 s. Stored in the ride's feed.

3Driver, ≤ 4 s

Pick up

The game's inbox poll brings it; the CB panel shows who paid for what.

4Driver, while driving

Apply

Night / day at once; a hole goes on the road ahead. Paused? It waits until the driver is back on the road.

5Driver → server

Stamp

The game reports when and where: game time, road position, lane offset – or that there was no room.

6Every spectator

Replay

The stamp reaches every watch page; the hole is put exactly there, so every replay – now or in a month – has it.

Why the stamp matters

A natural hole is a pure function of the road chunk it is in, so everyone who rebuilds the road gets it. A paid hole is not – it exists because someone paid while the driver was at a particular place. The driver's game therefore decides where it goes and reports it back once ({t, s, lat}, first report wins). Every watch page installs the same hole in the same chunk, replacing that chunk's own roll, and rebuilds the chunk if it is already on screen. The other cars on the driver's road steer around it like any hole.

Night and day need no stamp in the replay

The driver's clock is in every sample, so a replay simply shows the hour the driver had – including the jump a NIGHT donation caused. The stamp is still sent: it tells the spectator who paid that the driver's game really did it (✓ on the road in the feed), and when.

Chat

A CB radio in the cab

Spectator side

  • Sign in with a Solana wallet on the watch pageThe same sign-in as the landing and the lobby (one session for every page). On a split deployment the message names the game's host – ride.<domain> – so the wallet sees its own page's domain.
  • 140 characters, one line per 3 sControl and bidi characters are stripped and whitespace collapsed; everything is shown as text, never as HTML.
  • The feed is real time, the picture is delayedMessages appear in everyone's feed within a poll (≤ 4 s); the replay itself trails by ~25 s – like any stream.

Driver side

  • The CB panelTop left, out of the mirrors' way: the last four lines, each fading after 25 s, and a header with how many are watching, the message count and the mock total.
  • Toasts for effects“⚠ 7xKX…gAsU opened a sinkhole ahead – 1.0 km ahead, in your lane”, “🌙 … turned the day into night”.
  • Nothing to typeThe driver cannot post into their own ride's feed (403) – their hands are on the wheel.

Feed cursors

Event ids are numbered by the database at insert, and a slow insert can commit after a faster later one. So a client moves its after cursor only past events at least 3 s old, and drops repeats by id. Stamps arrive seconds or minutes after an event was posted – after the cursor has moved on – which is what since is for: each poll asks for stamps from 5 s before the previous poll's server time.

Honest limits

What this build does not do yet

  • Donations are a mock.Nothing is charged; the amounts are trusted as clicked. Real tips need a wallet transfer that the server credits only once the chain shows it (features plan F1 + the treasury watcher F8), and the effect should fire only after that.
  • Traffic is not in the replay.A spectator's road is empty. A crash into another vehicle shows as the truck stopping with a hull drop, not the car it hit. Recording nearby traffic would fix it.
  • Paid obstacles can hurt a paid ride.That is the point of the feature, but it is also a griefing lever. The 20 s window and the prices are the controls – set them deliberately before real money flows.
  • The seed of a live ride is public.Spectators need it to rebuild the road. A future seed commitment (plan F6) would have to reveal at the start of a ride, not at its end.
  • Replays expire with their recording.After 90 days (TELEMETRY_RETENTION_DAYS) the recording is swept; the feed stays, the replay says no recording was kept.
  • Not yet tried with a real Phantom on the live game host.The sign-in message names ride.<domain> there (checked locally on split hosts); a real wallet's domain check on production is still to be seen.
