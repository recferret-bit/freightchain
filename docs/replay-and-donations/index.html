Replays & Donations

Any ride can be watched – live while it is on the road, or as a replay once it has ended. Nothing is filmed: the watch page rebuilds the driver's road from the ride's seed and replays the same 10-per-second recording that verified play already uploads. Signed-in spectators can write to the driver and send donations that change the driver's ride – night, day, or a hole in the road ahead.

money MOCK or devnet SOL · a feature flag live view ~25 s behind the driver effects 1 per 20 s per ride · production: free rides only replays kept 90 days · the feed for good transport polling · no WebSockets
The path of one watched ride

From the driver's wheel to a spectator's screen

1Driver's browser

Record

Every online ride records the truck 10 times a second (verified play). Three columns were added for spectators: the hour of the day, the cab's switches and the controls in use.

2Every 15 s of driving

Upload

The batch is sealed, hashed, chained and stored by the server – the same upload the verdict judges. There is no second stream.

3Watch page, every 4 s

Poll

GET /api/watch/:id returns the ride, the next batches the playhead needs, and the feed since the last poll.

4Every frame

Replay

The road is rebuilt from the seed + route, its holes come from the server's obstacle plan; the truck's state is interpolated from the samples. Nothing is simulated.

5Signed in

Talk & donate

Chat lines and donations go into the ride's feed; the driver's game picks them up, shows them and applies the effects.

What a replay is

A rebuilt road and a recorded truck

The road is endless but deterministic: its whole layout – patterns, junctions, traffic lights, roadside props, the natural holes – is a pure function of the ride's seed and its route. The watch page gets both from the server, builds its own copy of the driver's road – its natural holes taken from the server's own obstacle plan (GET /api/watch/:id/road), never from anything the driver's game reported – and then writes the truck's state every frame from the recording instead of running the physics. Gauges, sway, the trailer, wipers, dust and lights all react exactly as they do for the driver, because they read the same state.

The traffic is replayed too: the driver's game records the nearest vehicles (up to 16 within 400 m – type, colour, position, heading, brake lights, sirens) twice a second beside each batch. That track sits outside the batch's fingerprint and is never judged; one that is too big or malformed is dropped, never refused. The replay draws the same vehicles and interpolates them. Recordings from before this have an empty road.

windscreen cracks, debris the radio the raw key presses – their millisecond timing is what the verdict's human-timing check measures, so they are never published

What spectators DO see of the driver's hands is the inputs column: one bit per control (gas, brake, steer, clutch, gears, the key, the lights, wipers, washer, heater, radio, pause, mute – W and ↑ are the same bit) plus one for clicked on the dash, set when the control was held at that sample or pressed since the one before, so a tap shorter than a tenth of a second still shows. The replay lights them as flashing yellow borders: a dash button is outlined where it sits (a click adds a pointer and a ring), keys show as caps in the lower left of the windscreen – Q W E / A S D / ⇧ always there, the others while lit. A fast replay never skips one: every sample the playhead passed in a frame counts, and a light fades over half a second of wall time. In the example, 65537 = gas held + the headlights switched. The bits never change meaning; new controls are appended (client/js/input-bits.js).

The holes are the server's

GET /api/watch/:id/road

The server rebuilds every ride's road from its seed (server/src/domain/road, a port of the game's generator that a drift test holds to it) and serves the list of its sinkholes – the obstacle plan – once the ride has started, up to 10 km per request. The driver's game draws and drives over those holes (client/js/road-plan.js), and so does every replay: what a spectator sees in the road is the server's plan, not the driver's copy. A game that took its holes out shows an empty road to its driver – the replay still has the holes in it.

Each watched batch also carries road: what the recorded truck did at the planned holes in it – hit, avoided, ignored (driven through at speed with none of the damage the game takes) – with the hull the game takes there and the hull the recording lost. These are facts about public samples on a public plan; the verdict (obstacle-ignored) uses the same measurement.

Live, behind, and replay

Following a live ride

● live
  • Target: 25 s behind the newest recorded secondOne 15 s batch plus its upload, with room for a late one.LIVE_LAG = 25 · client/js/watch.js
  • It eases, it doesn't jumpPlayback runs at 0.7× – 1.5× to settle on the target; only more than 60 s off makes it jump.

Online and history

Who is here, what is on the road, what can be replayed

The landing's chip and the lobby's header say N online · M on the road; LIVE NOW and the lobby's LIVE tab list every ride on the air; the lobby's REPLAYS tab and the landing's RECENT RIDES list every ride that ended.

On the road – accurately

  • On the air = a heartbeat in the last 150 sThe game beats every ~10 s (a paused or hidden tab keeps beating). Each ride shows how many watch it (👁) and its signal: live, PAUSED, or WEAK SIGNAL after 40 s without a beat.HB_TIMEOUT_MS · WEAK_SIGNAL_SECONDS
  • Abandoned rides are closedA ride silent for the timeout can never resume – its next heartbeat would fail it – so the stale-ride sweeper ends it (every 30 s) as failed · heartbeat lost, shown as “signal lost”. It used to stay “running” until its wallet started another ride. Closing the game tab ends the ride at once (a beacon to /end: abandoned); the sweeper is the fallback for a crash or a lost network.
  • Join▶ WATCH on any row, or WATCH A RANDOM ONE in the lobby – sorted newest / most watched / furthest, filtered by route.

Who is online – a soft number

  • Counted from the pages' own pollsThe landing and the lobby send a random id with their live-feed poll, the watch page with its own; the server counts distinct ids for 30 s – browsing, watching, driving – and the signed-in wallets among them. A page that closes says so (pagehide → the same poll with leave=1), so it drops out at the next poll of the others, not 30 s later.
  • Hard to pump, easy to resetAt most 3 ids per address count (one office or family = up to 3). Kept in the app's memory: a deploy or restart starts from zero, and a second app instance would count separately.

Donations

Six buttons, three kinds of effect

How a donation is paid is a feature flag (LIVE_FREE_DONATIONS). Mock: the price is shown and added up, nothing is charged. Paid (production): every donation is a real devnet SOL transfer from the spectator's wallet to the driver's – the server builds it, the wallet signs and sends it, and the donation only exists once the server has seen it on chain. The platform never touches the money. A donation can carry a note of up to 80 characters.

💰 Tip 0.01 SOL

Money only. It shows on the driver's CB panel and in the feed. No effect on the ride.

not limited by the effect window – one per wallet per 3 s

🌙 Night 0.02 SOL

The driver's clock jumps to night (it fades in; the ride's own start hour is the server's and is untouched). Headlights matter from here on.

the replay follows from the clock column

☀ Day 0.02 SOL

The clock jumps back to day. Undoes a night – or a spectator's mercy.

the replay follows from the clock column

🕳 Potholes 0.02 SOL

Three small pits across the driver's lane. Each costs up to 1.8 % hull per axle at full speed – straddle them or slow down.

HOLES.cluster

🚧 Trench 0.03 SOL

A trench across the whole lane: up to 6 % hull per axle and 15 % of the speed at 100 km/h. Change lanes or slow right down.

HOLES.trench

⚠ Sinkhole 0.05 SOL

A lane-wide sinkhole with cones: up to 14 % hull per axle and 35 % of the speed at 100 km/h. Three axles go over it – at full speed it can wreck a damaged truck.

HOLES.sinkhole · damage ∝ (v / 28 m/s)², rear axles × 0.6
The life of one donation

Posted, applied, stamped, replayed

1Spectator

Post

Signed in, clicks SINKHOLE. POST /api/watch/:id/donations.

2Server

Check

Not your own ride, the ride is on the air, no other effect on it in the last 20 s. Stored in the ride's feed.

3Driver, ≤ 4 s

Pick up

The game's inbox poll brings it; the CB panel shows who paid for what.

4Driver, while driving

Apply

Night / day at once; a hole goes on the road ahead. Paused? It waits until the driver is back on the road.

5Driver → server

Stamp

The game reports when and where: game time, road position, lane offset – or that there was no room.

6Every spectator

Replay

The stamp reaches every watch page; the hole is put exactly there, so every replay – now or in a month – has it.

Why the stamp matters

A natural hole is a pure function of the road chunk it is in, so everyone who rebuilds the road gets it. A paid hole is not – it exists because someone paid while the driver was at a particular place. The driver's game therefore decides where it goes and reports it back once ({t, s, lat}, first report wins). Every watch page installs the same hole in the same chunk, next to that chunk's own roll – a paid hole is only ever put on a chunk without a natural hole, and never replaces one, so the server's plan stays whole – and rebuilds the chunk if it is already on screen. Paid holes are not part of the verdict: where they go is the driver's game's call. The other cars on the driver's road steer around it like any hole.

Night and day need no stamp in the replay

The driver's clock is in every sample, so a replay simply shows the hour the driver had – including the jump a NIGHT donation caused. The stamp is still sent: it tells the spectator who paid that the driver's game really did it (✓ on the road in the feed), and when.

Paid donations

Money that moved is never refused

With LIVE_FREE_DONATIONS=off a click doesn't post a donation – it opens an order. Nothing is in the feed until the transfer is seen on chain.

1Spectator

Order

Same checks as any post. The server answers with the price, the driver's wallet, a memo fc-donation:<order> and the unsigned transfer.

2Wallet

Pay

Phantom signs and sends the transfer – spectator → driver, devnet SOL. The platform never holds it.

3Server

Verify

From your wallet, to the driver, at least the price, the order's memo, signed, not failed. Not visible yet → “pending”, ask again.

4Server

Claim

One conditional update marks the order paid by this transaction (signatures are unique) – two tabs confirming at once make one donation.

5Feed

Deliver

A new feed event, with a link to the transfer on the explorer. If its effect can no longer happen, it arrives as a tip saying why.

  • The effect window is checked twice.When the order is made (so nobody pays for an effect that visibly can't happen – 429 with the wait) and again when it is confirmed. Another effect in between, effects switched off, or the ride ended: the money already reached the driver, so the donation is delivered as a tip and its text says what happened.
  • Why an order and not a pending feed line.Feed cursors move past ids a few seconds old; a line that only became visible minutes later would be skipped. A paid order becomes a brand-new event when it is confirmed.

Chat

A CB radio in the cab

Spectator side

  • Sign in with a Solana wallet on the watch pageThe same sign-in as the landing and the lobby (one session for every page). On a split deployment the message names the game's host – ride.<domain> – so the wallet sees its own page's domain.
  • 140 characters, one line per 3 sControl and bidi characters are stripped and whitespace collapsed; everything is shown as text, never as HTML.
  • The feed is real time, the picture is delayedMessages appear in everyone's feed within a poll (≤ 4 s); the replay itself trails by ~25 s – like any stream.

Driver side

  • The CB panelTop left, out of the mirrors' way: the last four lines, each fading after 25 s, and a header with how many are watching, the message count and the total donated. K or a click on the header folds it away (remembered).
  • MuteEvery spectator line has a mute button. A muted wallet can't post on that ride any more, its chat lines vanish from every feed and its donation notes are blanked – an obstacle it already paid for stays on the road (replays need it).
  • Toasts for effects“⚠ 7xKX…gAsU opened a sinkhole ahead – 1.0 km ahead, in your lane”, “🌙 … turned the day into night”.
  • Nothing to typeThe driver cannot post into their own ride's feed (403) – their hands are on the wheel.

Feed cursors

Event ids are numbered by the database at insert, and a slow insert can commit after a faster later one. So a client moves its after cursor only past events at least 3 s old, and drops repeats by id. Stamps arrive seconds or minutes after an event was posted – after the cursor has moved on – which is what since is for: each poll asks for stamps from 5 s before the previous poll's server time.

Honest limits

What this build does not do yet

  • Paid donations are devnet SOL, confirmed by the spectator's own page.If the page is closed between paying and confirming, the money reached the driver but the donation never shows: there is no chain watcher for donations yet (features plan F8) and no "reconcile" button. The order stays pending.
  • Mock donations still exist as a switch.LIVE_FREE_DONATIONS=on makes sabotage free again – keep it off wherever paid rides are played. Production's default is off, and effects are limited to free rides.
  • The traffic in a replay is recorded at 2 Hz.Smooth enough to watch, not a physics record: the nearest 16 within 400 m, interpolated – a car further away or a very sharp manoeuvre can look simplified.
  • The seed of a live ride is public.Spectators need it to rebuild the road. A future seed commitment (plan F6) would have to reveal at the start of a ride, not at its end.
  • Replays expire with their recording.After 90 days (TELEMETRY_RETENTION_DAYS) the recording is swept; the feed stays, the replay says no recording was kept.
  • Not yet tried with a real Phantom on the live game host.The sign-in message names ride.<domain> there (checked locally on split hosts); a real wallet's domain check on production is still to be seen.
