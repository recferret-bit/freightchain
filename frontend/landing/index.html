OPERATOR-FUNDED POOLS & EXTRA ATTEMPTS

We and sponsors fund the pool (F = B + S > 50%), and paid tickets ($5 1 h · $15 4 h · $50 8 h) buy extra ranked attempts beside free weekly entry (AMOE). In the weekly contest (T = F + P + C), a capped bonus P = min(40% R_prev, $2,500, 90% F) keeps player funding below 50% (≤ 47.4%). We fund the pool, so it runs without a minimum ticket goal (unlike legacy devnet v3's 20 tickets of $5), and paid tickets are non-refundable once bought (unused expire at cut-off). The pot splits 85% weekly boards (1 h 25% · 4 h 30% · 8 h 45%; 80% top-10 + 20% clean finishers, equal tie-splitting, 30% free-ticket cap), 5% rollover, and 10% Season Final across 7 weeks.

ABOUT

Who built it, and why the game is a long drive. What is done, and what was cut, is on the trip sheet above.

WHY A TRUCK

Desert Bus (1995) was an eight-hour drive from Tucson to Las Vegas at 45 mph, for one point. People finished it, and other people watched. Freight Chain is that drive in a browser: you hold the wheel, the server keeps the clock, and a verified shift competes on skill boards.

WHO

One person built it. The cab, the routes and the weather came first. Sign-in, the lobby, live watch and the Anchor programs were built around them for the Colosseum Crypto World's Fair.

Devnet today; the Nov 2–17, 2026 Devnet Pre-season launches with a $500 USDC organizer-funded prize pool and 100,000 TEN4 airdrop points ahead of Season 1 mainnet (Jan 2027). TEN4 is a separate capped coin (100M supply) minted by verified paid checkpoints; until TGE (Mar 2027) it is tracked as ledger points. Pool rules are in the whitepaper.

FAQ

Plain answers for a first ride and for a wallet.

PLAY FREE RIDE NOW starts the real EASY ride. No wallet: an anonymous session, and that progress does not merge into a later sign-in. Training rides are unlimited. A ticket, and the weekly granted shift, need a sign-in with Phantom, Solflare, or QR phone pairing.

Training is free and unlimited. It is not ranked and it is not a prize entry. Every eligible wallet also gets one granted paid ticket a week (default 1 hour, AMOE; 30% free-ticket prize cap per board). A paid shift is a ticket into a pool. Those shifts are marked soon.

Nothing is for sale to start. Badges are soulbound, and we pay the mint. Even the Nov 2–17, 2026 Devnet Pre-season ($500 USDC + 100k TEN4 points) requires zero purchase.

SOL / USDC is the ticket and the prize. TEN4 is a separate capped coin minted by verified paid checkpoints (100,000,000 fixed supply: 55M ride emission across halving eras at 20% / 50% / 100% checkpoints, 15M LP, 8M Merkle airdrop, 4M Convoy referral, 10M treasury, 8M team; burned for 9-slot Garage parts and stream effects). HONK is the chat-line verb. A ticket is one entry into one pool.

Finishers rank by verified driving skill (distance before first mistake, then fewer corrections, with equal tie-splitting). This is a devnet build: nothing on it is real money, and no prize has been paid out yet. Our first prize event is the Nov 2–17, 2026 Devnet Pre-season ($500 USDC organizer-funded 1 h board + 100k TEN4 points), ahead of Season 1 mainnet in Jan 2027. The split on this page is the rule a pool publishes.

The vault is a program-owned account. We & sponsors fund the pool (> 50% base) and optional tickets (< 50% cap) buy extra attempts, so a pool never waits to fill a ticket goal (85% weekly boards · 5% rollover · 10% Season Final). Purchases are final & non-refundable (unused expire at start cut-off; refunded only if cancelled or unsettled after 14 days). The escrow, in the whitepaper.

The server keeps the clock. Speed is capped at about 100 km/h, so the finish is a distance. Reach the line and it is complete. A crash fails it. Quit and it is abandoned.

A live cab is about 25 seconds behind, and a finished ride can be replayed. Neither needs a wallet. Driving needs a desktop keyboard. A phone can watch, and it can tip a driver with the Solana Pay code.

No download. A desktop browser and a keyboard. Phantom, Solflare, or a phone wallet via QR pairing only when you want a counted ride or a ticket.

It is a driving game with skill-contest prize pools where operator + sponsor base funding exceeds player-derived bonus funding (< 50% player share). Free rides count on the leaderboard and toward badges. A paid pool pays places by verified driving skill. It is not a wage.
