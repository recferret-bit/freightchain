A truck, an endless road and a clock the server keeps. Pay for a 1, 4 or 8 hour shift, finish it, and your entry competes for one weekly prize pool.

WATCH LIVE

Devnet build. Paid rides use devnet SOL, nothing here is real money.

HOW IT WORKS

  1. PayPick a shift. SOL at the live rate, checked on chain by the server.
  2. DriveThe full time. The server counts it and caps your speed. Pause is honest, backgrounding earns nothing.
  3. FinishThe server marks the finish, not your browser.
  4. ShareEvery entry goes into one weekly pool. Finishers compete for it.

WHY

Desert Bus (1995) was a game about driving a bus across a desert in real time. Nobody should have finished it, and people did. The scarce thing is attention, so that is what this sells.

On cheating: the server keeps the clock and the speed limit. A modified client can still lie inside that limit. Deterministic replay is the next step, not built yet.
