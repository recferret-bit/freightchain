**1/**
FreightChain is a browser long-haul truck game on Solana.
You drive the cab yourself. The road never ends. Your shift does. One mistake ends it.
Here's how it works and how it makes money 🧵

**2/**
The problem: most web3 games ask for a wallet before you've had any fun, so players leave at the door.
FreightChain flips that. 10 free rides, no wallet, opens in a browser tab.

**3/**
The hook: in 1995, Desert Bus made you drive 8 real hours for 1 point.
We kept the endurance and replaced the point with a weekly prize pool held in an escrow program on Solana.

**4/**
Every ride is verified. Prizes are paid by the program, not by us.
Players don't have to trust the dev. They can check it themselves.

**5/**
Business model:
- Paid entries into the weekly pool, with a small protocol fee
- Sponsored routes and branded trucks
- Yield on pooled funds while they sit in escrow
The free rides stay free and feed the paid side.

**6/**
Why it can grow: browser casual games reach players with one link, with no install and no app store.
Solana makes small, fast, cheap payouts realistic every week.
