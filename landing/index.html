THE DASH · HOVER A CARD

IGNITION KEY

Off, on, start: hold to crank. I starts, O stops.

GEARBOX

Seven gears, clutch in to shift. Lug it and it stalls; redline it and it strains.

WARNING LAMPS

Amber: watch it. Red: fix it now. Blinking: seconds left.

LIGHTS & HEAT

L headlights, N night, H heater. All of it drains the battery.

PARK, WIPERS, WASH

B parking brake is on at every start. R wipers, hold X to wash.

TRIP & RADIO

Trip km, shift time, outside °C. T radio: one song for every cab at once.

ABOUT

Freight Chain sells proven time-on-road. The truck-driving sim is the first title.

WHY A TRUCK

Desert Bus (1995) was an eight-hour drive from Tucson to Las Vegas at 45 mph for one point. Nobody should have finished it, and people did – and other people watched. The scarce thing was attention. This is that, with the finish decided by a server and the pot in SOL.

BUILT · CUT · NEXT

  • Built: the sim, Sign-In With Solana, server-timed rides, verified play, live streams and replays, the escrow program and its server mirror, garage, promo codes, analytics, this page.
  • Cut on purpose: performance upgrades, mobile / Telegram, paid ads before a growth hire, a token, mainnet before legals, a penetration test and a load test.
  • Next: the program on devnet with real pools, a four-seat crew (CPO / COO, QA, community, content), a load test, a penetration test and legals, mainnet in December.

THE STACK

Vanilla ES modules + three.js in the browser, no bundler. NestJS + PostgreSQL on the server. An Anchor (Rust) escrow program, one vault per pool. Phantom / Solflare, SOL/USD from Coinbase then CoinGecko. Solo build since mid-September 2026, entered in the Colosseum Crypto World's Fair; the driving sim predates it and is disclosed in the README.
