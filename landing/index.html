THE DASH · HOVER A CARD

IGNITION KEY

Off, on, start: hold to crank. I starts, O stops.

GEARBOX

Seven gears, clutch in to shift. Lug it and it stalls; redline it and it strains.

WARNING LAMPS

Amber: watch it. Red: fix it now. Blinking: seconds left.

LIGHTS, HEAT & COLD

L headlights, C cab light. H melts frost, J clears fog on a warm route. Both drain the battery.

PARK, WIPERS, WASH

ABOUT

Who built it, and why the game is a long drive. What is done, and what was cut, is on the trip sheet above.

WHY A TRUCK

Desert Bus (1995) was an eight-hour drive from Tucson to Las Vegas at 45 mph, for one point. People finished it, and other people watched. Freight Chain is that drive in a browser: you hold the wheel, the server keeps the clock, and a finished shift can rank in a devnet pool.

WHO

One person built it. The cab, the routes and the weather came first. Sign-in, the lobby, live watch and the Anchor program were built around them for the Colosseum Crypto World's Fair.

Devnet only. There is no token, and no prize has been paid. The rules of a pool are in the whitepaper.

FAQ

Plain answers for a first ride and for a wallet.

A practice ride needs no account and is not recorded. The 10 signup rides, the daily refill and a ticket need a sign-in with Phantom or Solflare.

Practice is free. A new wallet gets 10 free rides, then a daily refill up to 5, and a promo code can add more. A paid shift is a ticket into a pool. Those shifts are marked soon.

Nothing is for sale to start. Badges are soulbound, and we pay the mint.

There is no token. A ticket is one entry into one pool. SOL is the entry and the prize.

Finishers rank by verified distance. This is a devnet build: nothing on it is real money, and no prize has been paid out. The split on this page is the rule a pool publishes.

The vault is an account the program owns. It holds the fees until the pool pays or refunds. Miss the ticket goal and every entry comes back. The escrow, in the whitepaper.

The server keeps the clock. Speed is capped at about 100 km/h, so the finish is a distance. Reach the line and it is complete. A crash fails it. Quit and it is abandoned.

A live cab is about 25 seconds behind, and a finished ride can be replayed. Neither needs a wallet. Driving needs a desktop keyboard. A phone can watch, and it can tip a driver with the Solana Pay code.

No download. A desktop browser and a keyboard. Phantom or Solflare only when you want a counted ride or a ticket.

It is a driving game with optional prize pools. Free rides count on the leaderboard and toward badges. A paid pool pays places by verified distance. It is not a wage.
