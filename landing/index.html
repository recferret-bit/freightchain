CORE MECHANICS & THE LOOP

It is Desert Bus with money on the line: the only skill that pays is staying on the road, awake, for the whole shift.

  1. 1BUY A SHIFTFree test ride, or $5 / $15 / $50 in SOL at the live rate. The server checks the transfer on chain and books the entry into this week's pool.
  2. 2DRIVE ALL OF ITEngine off at the depot. Start it, release the parking brake, work the clutch, pull onto the highway. Speed is governed at 100 km/h, so time on the road is the only way to make distance.
  3. 3SERVER CALLS ITHeartbeats every 10 s carry time and distance. Full time and full distance: COMPLETED. Quit early: ABANDONED. Hull gone, head-on, dead signal: FAILED.
  4. 4SHARE THE POOLEvery paid entry grows one weekly pot. Finishers compete for the 50% draw and the top-20 distance prize. Monday 00:00 UTC it rolls over.

MANUAL GEARBOX

7 gears, clutch, stalls, launch slip. Lug it and it dies; redline it and the STRAIN lamp charges until the drivetrain gives out.

HULL & TRAFFIC

Poles, guardrails, cacti and other trucks are solid. Scratches and bumps eat hull; a direct hit ends the shift. IDM traffic, crossroads with lights, multi-lane merges.

WEATHER & ENGINE

A −22 °C Trans-Siberian start means a frosted screen, a cold engine with less power, and wear if you rev it. Heater, wipers, washer, battery – all real.

DAY & NIGHT

The clock runs. Headlights, lamp posts, dazzle from oncoming traffic, a cab light. Backgrounding the tab pauses the shift – it earns nothing.

HOLES, SIGNS, STRAIN

Five kinds of road damage with warning signs; straddle a pothole or lose hull and speed. Driving against the parking brake cooks the brakes.

FIVE ROUTES

Same rules, different roads and weather:

  • Route 66 · USA
  • Stuart Hwy · Australia
  • Trans-Siberian · Russia
  • Brenner Pass · Alps
  • Pan-American · Atacama

REALTIME DAYTIME

The clock runs while you drive (×60 by default: a minute of driving is an hour of sky). Each route has its own sunrise, sunset and twilight – Trans-Siberian gets six hours of low sun, Atacama the brightest stars. At night the world is lit only by what has a lamp: your headlights, the lamp posts, oncoming traffic that dazzles you.

THE DASH · HOVER A CARD

IGNITION KEY

Three positions. OFF: nothing works – gauges dead, even the brake pedal. ON: a one-second sweep and bulb check, instruments live, battery draining. START: hold to crank. I is a one-tap start, O kills it.

GEARBOX

Seven gears, clutch in to shift (Shift + E/Q). Every gear pulls in a band: lug a tall one at a crawl and it stalls, sit on the redline and STRAIN charges. Q from neutral is reverse – stop first.

WARNING LAMPS

Eight lamps. Amber means watch it, red means fix it now, blinking red means seconds left. COLD cuts power until the engine warms; OFF ROAD gives you five seconds; BRAKES is what driving against the parking brake buys you.

LIGHTS & HEAT

L HEAD: at night the only thing lighting the road. N NIGHT jumps the clock. H HEAT thaws the frost from the vents outward and pulls heat out of the engine. All of it drains the battery with the engine off.

PARK, WIPERS, WASH

B PARK is a spring brake: ON at every start, holds with the engine off, holds against thrust – and cooks the brakes if you fight it. R wipers, hold X to wash. Dry blades barely clear the dust; wipers never clear frost.

TRIP & RADIO

Trip km, shift time over the minimum, outside °C – the server's clock is the one that counts. T radio: five stations of procedurally generated songs on a wall-clock broadcast, so every cab hears the same song at the same moment.

IN THE GAME

Rules of the garage: cosmetics and comfort, never the clock and never the speed. The governor, the minimum time and the distance bar are the same for a golden wheel and a stock one, so a paid part is never pay-to-win. Anything that changes physics (the splitter, the radiator) has to live in the server simulation before it can be sold – that is the replay core on the roadmap, not a client flag. Trucks and parts would be ordinary Solana tokens you can trade; the game only reads what you hold.

HOW THE ESCROW WORKS

  • The program holds the money, not us. A ticket goes straight into the pool’s vault – a program-derived account no one has a key to. Only the program’s rules move it.
  • The rules are fixed on day one. Price, minimum riders, deadlines, the split and the treasuries are written when the pool is created; no instruction edits them.
  • Missed goal = every lamport back. Too few riders by the deadline and the pool fails: anyone can trigger the refunds, the money lands with each buyer.
  • A live pool splits as declared – e.g. 70% prizes (payouts capped on chain), 20% seeds the next pool, 10% fees, to the treasuries the pool named.

Program written and tested; devnet deploy next. Not audited, not on mainnet.

WHAT THE SERVER CHECKS

THE CLOCK

Driven seconds come from heartbeats, capped by wall time. Honest pauses earn nothing. A gap over 150 s fails the ride.

THE SPEED

Reported distance may grow at most 28 m/s × 1.2 between beats. Faster than the truck can go = FAILED, not a high score.

THE TICKET

A ride spends one ticket of a live pool, under a lock – two tabs can’t spend it twice. Tickets are credited from the program’s events; the server never holds the money.

THE VERDICT

Completed / abandoned / failed is decided server-side and shown on the result screen. The client only reports.

Said plainly: this is server-timed telemetry, not proof. A modified client can still lie inside the speed bound. Night, hull and route are client-asserted and only unlock badges – they never move lamports. Next is a fixed-tick, fixed-point replay of the input log, so a paid finish is re-simulated, not reported. The 50% grand prize is a draw and the 20% top-20 is a skill prize; the draw is not live and prizes are not paid in this build.

ABOUT

Freight Chain sells proven time-on-road. Desert Run is the first title.

WHY A TRUCK

Desert Bus (1995) was an eight-hour drive from Tucson to Las Vegas at 45 mph for one point. Nobody should have finished it, and people did – and other people watched. The scarce thing was attention. This is that, with the finish decided by a server and the pot in SOL.

BUILT · CUT · NEXT

  • Built: the sim, Sign-In With Solana, server-timed rides, on-chain payment check, weekly pool accounting, live feed, boards, badges, this page.
  • Cut on purpose: Anchor escrow (memo-checked transfer instead), truck upgrades, mint-on-unlock, Telegram, mainnet.
  • Next: the draw and payouts, deterministic replay, a share page per ride.

THE STACK

Vanilla ES modules + three.js in the browser, no bundler. NestJS + SQLite on the server. Phantom / Solflare, System transfer + Memo, SOL/USD from Coinbase then CoinGecko. Solo build for the Colosseum Crypto World's Fair, Sep–Oct 2026; the driving sim predates it and is disclosed in the README.
