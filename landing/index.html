THE DASH · HOVER A CARD

IGNITION KEY

Off, on, start: hold to crank. I starts, O stops.

GEARBOX

Seven gears, clutch in to shift. Lug it and it stalls; redline it and it strains.

WARNING LAMPS

Amber: watch it. Red: fix it now. Blinking: seconds left.

LIGHTS, HEAT & COLD

L headlights, C cab light. H melts frost, J clears fog on a warm route. Both drain the battery.

PARK, WIPERS, WASH

B parking brake is on at every start. R wipers, hold X to wash.

TRIP & RADIO

Trip km, shift time, outside °C. T radio: one song for every cab at once.

ABOUT

Freight Chain sells proven time-on-road. The truck-driving sim is the first title.

WHY A TRUCK

Desert Bus (1995) was an eight-hour drive from Tucson to Las Vegas at 45 mph for one point. Nobody should have finished it, and people did – and other people watched. The scarce thing was attention. This is that, with the finish decided by a server and the pot in SOL.

BUILT · CUT · NEXT

  • Built: the sim, 1-click Native SIWS, SNS (.sol) lookup, Anchor v3 escrow with JitoSOL yield & Attestation PDAs, per-km Pledge PDAs, soulbound cNFT badges, Solana Pay, Blinks, server-timed rides, verified play, live streams, garage, promo codes.
  • Cut on purpose: performance upgrades, mobile driving before touch controls, paid ads before a growth hire, a token, mainnet before legals and audit.
  • Next: program audit and mainnet migration, a four-seat crew (CPO / COO, QA, community, content), convoy team drives, mobile driving controls.

THE STACK

Vanilla ES modules + three.js in the browser, no bundler, zero runtime npm dependencies. NestJS + PostgreSQL on the server (Clean Architecture). An Anchor v3 (Rust) escrow program with vault PDAs, JitoSOL liquid staking yield, and Attestation gating. Native 1-click SIWS, SPL Name Service PDA resolution, Metaplex Bubblegum v2 cNFT badges, Solana Pay, and Blinks. Solo build entered in the Colosseum Crypto World's Fair.
