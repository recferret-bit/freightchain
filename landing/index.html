CORE MECHANICS & THE LOOP

It is Desert Bus with money on the line: the only skill that pays is staying on the road, awake, for the whole shift.

  1. 1BUY A SHIFTFree test ride, or $5 / $15 / $50 in SOL at the live rate. The server checks the transfer on chain and books the entry into this week's pool.
  2. 2DRIVE ALL OF ITEngine off at the depot. Start it, release the parking brake, work the clutch, pull onto the highway. Speed is governed at 100 km/h, so time on the road is the only way to make distance.
  3. 3SERVER CALLS ITHeartbeats every 10 s carry time and distance. Full time and full distance: COMPLETED. Quit early: ABANDONED. Hull gone, head-on, dead signal: FAILED.
  4. 4SHARE THE POOLEvery paid entry grows one weekly pot. Finishers compete for the 50% draw and the top-20 distance prize. Monday 00:00 UTC it rolls over.

MANUAL GEARBOX

7 gears, clutch, stalls, launch slip. Lug it and it dies; redline it and the STRAIN lamp charges until the drivetrain gives out.

HULL & TRAFFIC

Poles, guardrails, cacti and other trucks are solid. Scratches and bumps eat hull; a direct hit ends the shift. IDM traffic, crossroads with lights, multi-lane merges.

WEATHER & ENGINE

A −22 °C Trans-Siberian start means a frosted screen, a cold engine with less power, and wear if you rev it. Heater, wipers, washer, battery – all real.

DAY & NIGHT

The clock runs. Headlights, lamp posts, dazzle from oncoming traffic, a cab light. Backgrounding the tab pauses the shift – it earns nothing.

HOLES, SIGNS, STRAIN

Five kinds of road damage with warning signs; straddle a pothole or lose hull and speed. Driving against the parking brake cooks the brakes.

WHAT THE SERVER CHECKS

THE CLOCK

Driven seconds come from heartbeats, capped by wall time. Honest pauses earn nothing. A gap over 150 s fails the ride.

THE SPEED

Reported distance may grow at most 28 m/s × 1.2 between beats. Faster than the truck can go = FAILED, not a high score.

THE PAYMENT

A System transfer to the treasury of at least the quoted lamports, with the ride id as memo, from your wallet, confirmed. The signature is the primary key – no double credit.

THE VERDICT

Completed / abandoned / failed is decided server-side and shown on the result screen. The client only reports.

Said plainly: this is server-timed telemetry, not proof. A modified client can still lie inside the speed bound. Night, hull and route are client-asserted and only unlock badges – they never move lamports. Next is a fixed-tick, fixed-point replay of the input log, so a paid finish is re-simulated, not reported. The 50% grand prize is a draw and the 20% top-20 is a skill prize; the draw is not live and prizes are not paid in this build.

ABOUT

Freight Chain sells proven time-on-road. Desert Run is the first title.

WHY A TRUCK

Desert Bus (1995) was an eight-hour drive from Tucson to Las Vegas at 45 mph for one point. Nobody should have finished it, and people did – and other people watched. The scarce thing was attention. This is that, with the finish decided by a server and the pot in SOL.

BUILT · CUT · NEXT

  • Built: the sim, Sign-In With Solana, server-timed rides, on-chain payment check, weekly pool accounting, live feed, boards, badges, this page.
  • Cut on purpose: Anchor escrow (memo-checked transfer instead), truck upgrades, mint-on-unlock, Telegram, mainnet.
  • Next: the draw and payouts, deterministic replay, a share page per ride.

THE STACK

Vanilla ES modules + three.js in the browser, no bundler. NestJS + SQLite on the server. Phantom / Solflare, System transfer + Memo, SOL/USD from Coinbase then CoinGecko. Solo build for the Colosseum Crypto World's Fair, Sep–Oct 2026; the driving sim predates it and is disclosed in the README.
